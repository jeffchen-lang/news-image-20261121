A dense one-page infographic summing up Saturday 21 November 2026's world news — 40 stories in all. The lead is Bitcoin's crash to $55,130, its lowest since September 2024, with $2.67B of crypto liquidated in 24 hours; then Russia's overnight assault on Ukraine (400+ drones, 100+ missiles), two oil tankers struck near Oman, and ten more drawn feature stories — Gaza, a Brazil storm, a Finnish airspace probe, EU–Indonesia trade deals, a Basra power-station fire, a Banda Sea quake, a Russian cargo ship ablaze, troop losses, a ceasefire hope and a Denmark murder plot — plus 27 more stories, a six-number stat strip, a region bar and a world map with numbered pins. A date-check footer lists what was deliberately left out as prior-day news (the ICJ Myanmar verdict, Friday's market selloff, the Peru quake). `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte, every run.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
